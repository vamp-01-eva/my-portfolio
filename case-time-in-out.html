project overview

An internal attendance tool built during the DepEd ICT internship. Employees record time-in and time-out in the browser; administrators get one central attendance record instead of scattered manual entries.

The system digitizes the employee attendance log for daily office use. An employee opens the web app, records a time-in or time-out, and the entry lands in a central attendance record.

The build was part of the DepEd ICT Unit internship in Antipolo. It was made for internal administrative use, not as a public product.

problem statement

Attendance records were kept as manual entries. The office needed a digital record that was easier to complete, easier to keep in one place, and easier for administrators to review.

objectives

  1. Give employees a simple browser-based way to record time-in and time-out.
  2. Keep attendance entries in one central record.
  3. Give administrators a clear view of the day’s attendance.
  4. Reduce the manual work of collecting and checking attendance logs.
