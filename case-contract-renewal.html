project overview

A Google Workspace automation that keeps personnel contract information in one inventory, monitors renewal dates in a dashboard, and sends email notifications. Super Admin and School Admin roles control what each administrator can see.

The system connects Google Forms, Google Sheets, a web dashboard, and email notifications. A form submission becomes a row in the personnel inventory, the dashboard monitors contract and renewal details, and the email workflow notifies the people involved.

The build includes two administrator roles. A Super Admin sees the whole organization and can export records; a School Admin sees the school assigned to them. The dashboard groups records into all records, renewal alerts, and archives.

problem statement

Contract details and renewal dates were spread across separate records. Administrators needed one personnel inventory that showed which contracts were due and a repeatable way to notify the people involved.

objectives

  1. Collect personnel contract information through Google Forms.
  2. Keep the data in Google Sheets as the central inventory.
  3. Monitor contracts and renewal dates in a web dashboard.
  4. Send automated email notifications before a contract expires.
  5. Limit each administrator’s view to the records they are allowed to see.
