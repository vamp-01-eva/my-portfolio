project overview

A workflow automation that collects contract renewal information through Google Forms, keeps it in Google Sheets, monitors it in a web dashboard, and sends email notifications. Super Admin and School Admin roles control what each administrator can see.

The system connects Google Forms, Google Sheets, a web dashboard, and email notifications. A form submission becomes a row in the sheet, the dashboard monitors the renewal record, and the email workflow notifies the people involved.

The build includes two administrator roles. A Super Admin sees the whole organization and can export records; a School Admin sees the school assigned to them.

problem statement

Google Forms makes it easy to collect renewal information, but a form response on its own is hard to monitor. The office needed one database, a dashboard that showed what was due, role-based visibility, and a notification workflow.

objectives

  1. Collect contract renewal information through Google Forms.
  2. Keep the data in Google Sheets as the central database.
  3. Monitor renewals in a web dashboard.
  4. Send automated email notifications.
  5. Limit each administrator’s view to the records they are allowed to see.
