project overview

An internal dashboard built during the DepEd ICT internship. Teachers submit account requests, administrators review them, and every request keeps its status and activity history.

The dashboard centralizes teacher account requests for Google and Microsoft accounts. A request enters one queue, an administrator reviews it, and the request moves through a visible approval state.

The design includes multiple administrator roles and an activity log so the office can see who reviewed or changed a request. The build was part of the DepEd ICT Unit internship in Antipolo.

problem statement

Account requests arrived through different channels, and administrators needed different levels of access. The office needed one queue, a clear approval state, and a record of who did what.

objectives

  1. Collect Google and Microsoft account requests in one queue.
  2. Support multiple administrator roles.
  3. Make the approval workflow and request status visible.
  4. Keep an activity log for administrative review.
