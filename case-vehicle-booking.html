project overview

An internal booking tool built during the DepEd ICT internship. A request becomes a ticket, the ticket triggers an email notification, and the booking stays in one organized record.

The system organizes vehicle drop-off and pick-up requests in one web app. A requester submits the booking details, the app generates a ticket, and an email notification goes out.

The build was part of the DepEd ICT Unit internship in Antipolo. It was made for internal administrative use.

problem statement

Vehicle requests were handled as separate messages and manual notes. The office needed a booking record that could be tracked, a ticket for each request, and a notification that did not depend on someone re-typing the details.

objectives

  1. Accept drop-off and pick-up requests in one form.
  2. Generate a ticket for each booking.
  3. Send an email notification when a booking is made.
  4. Keep booking records organized and easy to track.
