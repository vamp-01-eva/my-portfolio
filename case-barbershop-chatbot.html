project overview

An AI customer support prototype for barbershops. It answers common questions about services and haircuts in English, Tagalog, or Taglish, keeps conversation context with thread identifiers, and logs the conversation in Google Sheets.

The chatbot is designed for customer inquiries on Facebook Messenger. A message goes through Make.com to the OpenAI Responses API, which answers with the help of a Google Docs knowledge base. The conversation is logged in Google Sheets.

The project is an independent prototype. The chat and FAQ flow is the current build; appointment booking is a planned extension, not a finished feature.

problem statement

Barbershop staff answer the same questions repeatedly, often in a mix of English, Tagalog, and Taglish. The shop needed a way to answer common questions quickly without losing the thread of the conversation.

objectives

  1. Answer common barbershop questions in English, Tagalog, and Taglish.
  2. Keep the chatbot’s instructions and knowledge in a Google Doc.
  3. Maintain conversation context with a thread identifier.
  4. Log conversations in Google Sheets for review.
  5. Leave room for an appointment booking workflow.
