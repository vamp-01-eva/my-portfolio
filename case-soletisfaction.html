project overview

A capstone project for a shoe reselling business. The system combines an online product catalog, point-of-sale transactions, inventory management, sales monitoring, and customer accounts in one platform.

Soletisfaction is an academic capstone project for a shoe reselling business. The system was designed to manage online sales, in-store point-of-sale transactions, inventory, pricing, and customer information in one place.

The project was built as a team capstone at STI College. The work covered front-end and back-end implementation, database management, and application testing.

problem statement

The business tracked online sales, in-store sales, and inventory separately. The manual process made it harder to see what was in stock, what had sold, and what the business had earned.

objectives

  1. Create one product catalog for the online store and the point of sale.
  2. Manage product and inventory records.
  3. Record point-of-sale transactions.
  4. Monitor sales and income.
  5. Manage customer accounts and status.
  6. Send email notifications and give administrators a dashboard.
