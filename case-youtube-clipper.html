project overview

The first stage of a shorts production pipeline. A form takes a YouTube URL and clip settings; the workflow downloads the source, transcribes it, asks Google Gemini to pick the strongest moments, cuts vertical clips, and uploads them to Google Drive.

The clipper is built in n8n. A form submission starts the job. The workflow downloads the source video, gets and parses the transcript, builds candidate windows, and sends them to Google Gemini to pick the strongest moments. The selected ranges are cut into vertical clips and uploaded to a Google Drive folder.

Clips are rendered without burned-in captions on purpose. Editing happens first; the Local Video Captioner adds styled captions afterward.

The project is a working local prototype. The workflow runs from the request form to the Drive upload.

The clipper and reclipper keep clips caption-free; the captioner adds captions after editing.

problem statement

Turning a long video into Shorts is repetitive: download, transcribe, find the moments worth keeping, cut them, reframe them, and upload the results. Doing each step by hand slows down a publishing schedule.

objectives

  1. Accept a YouTube URL and clip settings in one form.
  2. Download the source and get its transcript.
  3. Use the transcript to build candidate clip windows.
  4. Ask Google Gemini to pick the strongest moments.
  5. Cut vertical, caption-free clips.
  6. Upload the clips to Google Drive and build a job report.

my role and contributions

design
Designed the job flow from form submission through download, transcript, moment selection, cuts, and Drive delivery.
build
Built the n8n workflow, the request form, the clip settings, and the Drive upload path.
prompt
Wrote the moment-selection prompt and tuned it against the candidate windows and the requested focus.
testing
Tested the workflow end to end with sample videos and adjusted the cut and upload steps.

AI-assisted development tools were used for parts of the implementation. I reviewed the workflow, ran it against sample videos, and refined the result rather than treating the output as finished.

main features

  1. Request formTakes the YouTube URL, clip count, framing, length, hook card, progress bar, audio polish, watermark, and cookie setting.
  2. Transcript-driven windowsDownloads the source, gets the transcript, and builds candidate moments from it.
  3. AI moment pickingSends the candidate windows to Google Gemini and selects the strongest moments for the requested focus.
  4. Vertical cutsCuts the selected ranges into vertical clips with the chosen framing.
  5. Drive deliveryCreates a Drive folder and uploads the finished clips.
  6. Job reportBuilds a report for the run after the clips are uploaded.
