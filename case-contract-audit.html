project overview

An internal contract monitoring tool started during the DepEd ICT internship. It keeps personnel contract information and renewal dates in one place and uses email workflows to flag renewals. The build was not fully completed.

The system was designed to help administrators manage personnel contract information and monitor renewals. It combines a contract record, renewal tracking, and email notification workflows.

The project was partially developed during the internship. It is presented here as an in-progress build, not a completed deployment.

problem statement

Contract details and renewal dates were spread across separate records. Administrators needed a central view of which contracts were due and a repeatable way to notify the people involved.

objectives

  1. Keep personnel contract information in one record.
  2. Track renewal dates and the status of each contract.
  3. Use email workflows to notify the people involved.
  4. Give administrators a clearer monitoring view.
