project overview

An internal reservation tool built during the DepEd ICT internship. Room requests go into one schedule, and the booking triggers an email notification.

The system simplifies conference room scheduling by keeping reservations in one web app. A requester selects a room and a time, and the booking is recorded and sent by email.

The build was part of the DepEd ICT Unit internship in Antipolo. It was made for internal administrative use.

problem statement

Room requests were handled through separate messages and manual notes. The office needed one reservation record that showed what was booked, when, and by whom.

objectives

  1. Accept conference room reservations in one form.
  2. Keep room bookings in a central record.
  3. Send an email notification for each reservation.
  4. Make the room schedule easier to review and track.
